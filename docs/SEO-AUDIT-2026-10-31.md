Business type: local hospital (brick-and-mortar) | Market: Palanpur, Banaskantha | Languages: English, Gujarati, Hindi

## 1. Summary

Health score before today's fixes: **69/100** (heuristic, not a Google signal). Not measured: Core Web Vitals.
Local visibility and Google Business Profile completeness: not scored, because the profile dashboard was not available.

The site's technical base is sound. What holds it back is off-site: in the searches run for this audit, directories and competitors appeared and the hospital and its doctor did not. Those searches used a US-based, non-Google tool, so they are a signal to confirm from Palanpur rather than a measured Google ranking.

No one can guarantee a first position. Typical timelines: map results move 4-8 weeks after profile and listing fixes; competitive organic terms take 3-6 months of steady work.
