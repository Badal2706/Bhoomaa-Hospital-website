What "24x7" means here

Plenty of listings claim round-the-clock cover without saying what is actually behind it, so to be precise about this one:

  • Nursing staff are physically at the hospital overnight, with inpatients and ICU patients under observation
  • The doctor is on call and attends when needed
  • The ICU, ventilator, oxygen and X-ray are in the same building, not at a partner facility across town
  • OPD consulting hours are 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, seven days a week. Emergencies are not limited to those hours

Emergencies the hospital handles

Chest pain and cardiac events

ECG, echocardiography and Echo Doppler on site for rapid assessment.

Breathing difficulty

Severe asthma, pneumonia and COPD, with oxygen and ventilator support available.

Paralysis and brain haemorrhage

Neurological emergencies needing immediate observation and stabilisation.

Poisoning and snake bite

Round-the-clock management with ICU backup. More on this.

Severe malaria, dengue, jaundice

Fevers and infections that have progressed beyond outpatient treatment.

Diabetic emergencies

Very high or very low sugars needing urgent correction and monitoring.
