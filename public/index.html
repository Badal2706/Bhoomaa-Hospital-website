About BHOOMAA Medicare Hospital

Local care noteBehind Kotak Mahindra Bank · Ambika Society · Palanpur 385001

BHOOMAA Medicare Hospital & I.C.U. is a local healthcare facility in Palanpur, Gujarat, offering medical and critical care services for patients and families who need clear guidance and dependable support.

Conditions We Treat

Based on the hospital's supplied service list

Our hospital provides care across a range of medical and critical conditions, led by our consulting physician.

Cardiac Care

Care for heart attack and related cardiac conditions, including monitoring and diagnostics.

Diabetes Care

Ongoing diabetic care and management under a qualified diabetologist.

Emergency Medical Care

24/7 emergency care for urgent medical situations.

Respiratory Care

Treatment for asthma, allergy, pneumonia, tuberculosis and severe respiratory conditions.

Neurological & Critical Conditions

Care for paralysis, brain hemorrhage, coma and brain fever.

Medical care for poisoning and snake bite cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Consultation hours are 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. Emergency care is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Walk-in patients are welcome during consultation hours. Booking an appointment in advance simply helps reduce your waiting time. Emergencies are attended to immediately, at any hour.

Family members can visit admitted patients during regular hospital hours. ICU visits are restricted to specific times for patient safety and infection control. ICU visiting is generally permitted for a short time in the morning and evening, one attendant at a time. Please check with the ward staff on arrival.

The hospital does not operate its own ambulance service at present. In an emergency, please arrange local transport and call the hospital on 02742-255540 so our team can prepare for your arrival.

Insurance and TPA arrangements may vary, and cashless facilities may not be available for every scheme. Please call the hospital on 02742-255540 before your visit so the team can confirm what currently applies and help you plan accordingly.
